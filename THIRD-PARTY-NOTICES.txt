Optional FFmpeg command-line program
------------------------------------
FFmpeg is not embedded in the app or bundled in any of its packages (the
installer, the ZIP, the DMG, the AppImage, the Flatpak or the tar.gz). The app
can use a user-supplied ffmpeg or download a static LGPL build for Windows or
Linux directly from https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds/releases, or on a Mac
static ffmpeg and ffprobe builds (GPL v2 or later: they include x264, x265 and
LAME) from https://github.com/shaka-project/static-ffmpeg-binaries/releases.
That download retains upstream LICENSE/COPYING notices and a SOURCE.txt
record of the release URL and verified GitHub SHA-256 digest in the cache.
FFmpeg project and source: https://ffmpeg.org/ and https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git
The downloaded program is governed by its own accompanying licenses.
